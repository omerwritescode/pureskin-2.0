About PureSkin

What it is, how a scan works, the methods behind it and their limits, and who made it.

What PureSkin is

PureSkin turns a selfie and a short questionnaire into a Skin Profile: five scores (acne, redness, wrinkles, pigmentation and pore visibility), each shown as Low, Moderate or High, plus your skin type and whether your answers point to sensitive skin.

With an account, your profile builds a morning and evening routine from a catalog of real products. Conflicting active ingredients are never in the same morning or evening, and every product left out says why. A chat answers skincare questions from a small set of notes and your profile.

How a scan works

  1. A selfie. Take one with your camera, or upload a photo you already have: either works. PureSkin checks it straight away and asks for another if it can't be used.
  2. A few questions. Eight short steps about how your skin feels and reacts, and what you already use.
  3. Your Skin Report. Five scores, each Low, Moderate or High, and your skin type. Create an account to keep them and get your routine.

Your photo is analysed in memory and never saved on our servers. Your answers are scored in memory and never stored; PureSkin saves only the results, and only to an account.

How it works, and its limits

What each part does, and what it can't do.

Photo scores

Face landmarks (MediaPipe) and measurements written by hand (OpenCV and NumPy), taken on a 720 px copy of your photo once checks show it is sharp, bright enough, and has your face close enough and turned towards the camera.

Limits. Lighting and your camera change these scores. The acne score leans on your answers, because the photo's acne measurement wasn't reliable.

Skin type and sensitivity

From your answers, by written rules and a small word list for anything you type.

Limits. A model that guessed skin type from photos was trained, but on test photos kept apart from its training set it did no better than always picking the most common skin type (an accuracy of 0.309, against 0.31), so PureSkin doesn't use it.

Your routine

Rules decide which catalog products suit your skin: conflicting active ingredients are never in the same morning or evening, sensitive skin gets no strong actives or fragrance, and your avoid list and current products are respected. A ranker then puts the products the rules allow in order, by the ingredients they list.

Limits. The ranker's weights were set by hand and never tuned. PureSkin has no data on how any product worked for anyone, so nothing was trained on results.

The catalog

37 real products, each with the brand or shop page its ingredient list was copied from.

Limits. It isn't a full market: the products were chosen to fill the routine's steps, and a few steps have no product yet.

The chat

Answers from 24 notes written from public health pages (mostly MedlinePlus and the NIH) and PureSkin's own documents, together with your Skin Profile. Its language model runs on the same computer as the PureSkin server, and the chat stores nothing. Questions about warning signs, such as a possible allergic reaction, get a fixed reply that never reaches the model: it points you to a doctor or pharmacist, and in an emergency to 112.

Limits. No one has measured how often its answers match its notes.

What PureSkin isn't

  • A diagnosis

    The five scores are estimates from your photo and your answers, to guide your routine. Lighting and your camera change the photo-based scores, and they haven't been checked against a doctor's assessment. Your skin type and sensitivity come from your answers, not the photo.

  • Medical advice

    The chat answers from a small set of skincare notes and your Skin Profile, and its answers can be wrong or incomplete. For a skin problem or a reaction to a product, ask a doctor or pharmacist.

  • The final word on ingredients

    Ingredient lists are copied from brand pages and may differ by market or batch. Global ingredient list: the list comes from the brand's non-India website; the version sold in India may differ.

What isn't built

PureSkin is a student project, and parts of its plan aren't built yet:

  • paid plans: there's no Pricing page and no subscription;
  • reminders and notifications;
  • the planned skin visualiser;
  • a page for each saved scan, and a form to change your password;
  • analysis on your phone: the photo always goes to the PureSkin server, which analyses it in memory;
  • deployment: PureSkin runs on one computer, which serves the pages and runs the database and the chat's language model.
