Chat

PureSkin's chat answers from a small set of skincare notes and your Skin Profile. Its answers can be wrong or incomplete, and they are not medical advice. For a skin problem, a reaction to a product, a medicine, pregnancy or anything that worries you, ask a doctor or pharmacist. In an emergency, call 112.

Nothing you type is saved: the conversation is cleared when you leave or reload this page, and the chat only sees your last three questions and answers. For adults (18+), in English.

    Enter sends. Shift and Enter starts a new line. Escape stops an answer.
