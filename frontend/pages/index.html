How a scan works

  1. 1

    A selfie

    Take one with your camera, or upload a photo you already have: either works. PureSkin checks it straight away and asks for another if it can't be used.

  2. 2

    A few questions

    Eight short steps about how your skin feels and reacts, and what you already use. Most questions have a “Not sure” answer.

  3. 3

    Your Skin Report

    Five scores, each Low, Moderate or High, and your skin type. Create an account to keep them and get your routine.

Why this product, and not that one

Rules decide which products from the catalog suit your skin. Every step in your routine says why its product is there, and every product left out says why not.

What's kept, and what isn't

Kept, once you have an account

  • Your account. Your email address and name. Your password is stored only as a hash, never as you typed it.
  • Each scan you save. The five scores, your skin type, whether your answers point to sensitive skin, and the date.
  • A few tags. Picked from fixed lists: the kinds of products you use, the ingredients you want to avoid and your main goal. Never the words you typed.

It's kept in a database on the computer that runs PureSkin. The full list is on the Privacy page in Settings, where you can download your data or delete your account.

Not kept

  • Your photo. It's analysed in memory and never saved on our servers. During the scan it waits in your browser tab, and it's removed when the scan finishes or you close the tab.
  • Your answers. They're scored in memory and never stored, including anything you type. Only the results are saved.
  • Your chats. The chat stores nothing: no messages, answers or history. Its language model runs on the same computer as the PureSkin server, not on a cloud AI service.
  • Your routine. It's worked out again from your latest scan each time you open it.

No PureSkin page loads files from another website, and none has advertising or analytics scripts.

What PureSkin isn't

  • A diagnosis

    The five scores are estimates from your photo and your answers, to guide your routine. Lighting and your camera change the photo-based scores, and they haven't been checked against a doctor's assessment. Your skin type and sensitivity come from your answers, not the photo.

  • Medical advice

    The chat answers from a small set of skincare notes and your Skin Profile, and its answers can be wrong or incomplete. For a skin problem or a reaction to a product, ask a doctor or pharmacist.

  • The final word on ingredients

    Ingredient lists are copied from brand pages and may differ by market or batch. Global ingredient list: the list comes from the brand's non-India website; the version sold in India may differ.
