## Scope

The whole PureSkin frontend, redesigned, plus a new landing page at `/`. The landing page is Persuade; the scan funnel, auth and every /app page are Operate. Light theme only. Product truth, copy rules and contract tests: PRODUCT.md and CLAUDE.md. Built in user-approved units, each stopping for review at 360 px and 1440 px.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The Skin Profile as a paint-shop shade card: the five scores are named blades of one fan deck, each band is a labelled chip, and every left-out product carries a printed reason. It refuses the beauty landing (dewy model, product bottles, cream ground with a serif) and the clinical white page with percentages.

OWN-WORLD: Counter-grey ground (#eeeff0), white card-stock slips with a soft lift and a rivet hole, ink (#191a1c) for text and current states, deep brown (#3d261a) for the one action, five decorative skin chips (never data, never ordered by tone). Archivo only: expanded black headings, normal-width text, 87.5% condensed for chips. Scores are band strips: three labelled chips sized by their range.

STORY: A beginner sees what PureSkin reads (five named scores), that the photo is not kept, and that every product decision has a reason; they start a free scan.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Landing at 1440: a five-blade fan deck pinned at its rivet, centred, a macro photo of real skin under each name; below it a two-line headline, a three-line lede, "Start your free scan" in action brown with Log in and Sign up beside it, then the privacy line. Phones: copy first, then the deck closed into one upright strip card. Signature: the deck fans open once (1.1 s ease-out); a blade lifts on pointer hover; the only video (How a scan works) has a visible pause control and never plays under reduced motion.

FORM: Shade card (paint fan deck), position 1 on my ordered list: my own pick; the roll assigned the mosaic floor, and the user chose this one. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
